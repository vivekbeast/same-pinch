Same Pinch

What I built, the choices I made, and what I left out.

01 · The idea

Do two strangers notice the same thing?

1You see a photo and tap the first thing your eye goes to. Naming it is optional, in any language.

2A real player somewhere in India saw the same photo. Same spot, or the same meaning, is a Same Pinch.

3Every agreement is a human-verified label (the ESP Game idea): real data work, so a verified match can pay.

02 · The first minute

Play before you sign up.

A ten-second story shows the whole idea. Then three practice rounds against recorded players. No form until you've felt the game.

02 · The first minute

Practice teaches by doing.

Tap, then see their pin and their words, in their language. A one-line coach explains each result.

03 · A real round

The server decides. Then the reveal.

Your tap is stored first. Then your partner's pin, a heatmap of everyone's pinches, and a rematch chip if you've met before.

05 · After the pack

Every round, dealt back.

Cards flip over in 3D, XP fills, flame and badges pop. Three matches in a row: a hot hand, half the time for double XP.

06 · Fair by design

Paid for the work. Never for luck.

₹Money only for verified matches, scaled by answer quality on hidden test photos.

★Streaks, levels, the weekly board and the hot hand give XP and badges, never money. India's 2025 Online Gaming Act bans money games.

✓No deposits. Earned money never goes down. This build pays demo money.

06 · Fair by design

A ledger you can trust.

Append-only, kept in paise, written only by the server. Payouts are simulated until KYC and a payout partner exist.

07 · Every day

Think Like India.

Five prompts, one word each, graded against what everyone said. A shareable grid. XP only.

08 · Built for Indian phones

Plays on a patchy network.

Packs download ahead. Offline, your answer is saved and queued, and the reveal waits for the network. You keep playing.

11 · Choices

Trade-offs I made on purpose.

Recorded partners, not live matchmaking

No waiting room, it works offline, and every answer becomes a future partner.

Express + Postgres, not Firebase

Money needs real transactions and an append-only ledger.

Tap first, words optional

One thumb, a minute a pack, and it works whatever language you think in.

CSS 3D + Motion, not three.js

three.js alone would use the whole 150 KB JavaScript budget.

12 · Left out, on purpose

What isn't built yet.

–Sound prompts and voice input: designed in the docs, not built.

–Real UPI payouts: they need KYC and a payout partner. The ledger and payout flow run in demo mode.

–Live two-player rounds and an admin tool to review new word matches.

–Browser end-to-end tests and Lighthouse in CI: today CI runs unit and API tests plus the bundle budget.
